      About these numbers

      • March 1, 2026 is when Jerry's results dropped off and the drawing machine is believed to have changed, so the board counts from there unless you pick other dates.
      • Results up to November 4, 2026 were gathered from public results sites (Lottery Post, Lottery USA, Lottery Extreme, LottoNumbers, lottery.net) and checked against each other. Later drawings are typed in with the box at the top. The Michigan Lottery's own results are the official record.
      • Every number has the same chance in each drawing. These counts show what has happened, not what will happen: over time, any six numbers match about 0.77 a drawing, and the jackpot needs all six of 47, about 1 in 10.7 million.

      New drawings are added with the "Add a drawing" box near the top of the page. The Michigan Lottery's own results are the official record.
