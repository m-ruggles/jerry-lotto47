      About these numbers

      • Results up to November 4, 2026 were gathered from public results sites (Lottery Post, Lottery USA, Lottery Extreme, LottoNumbers) and checked against each other where they overlap. Later drawings are typed in by hand with the box at the top. Check any single draw against the Michigan Lottery before relying on it.
      • The March 1, 2026 dividing line is Jerry's suspected machine change. No public notice of an equipment change was found.
      • "1 in N" figures say how rarely a fair, perfectly random draw would produce a result at least that lopsided. A small sample can look lopsided by chance, so these are clues, not proof.
      • Matching more numbers on average still leaves the jackpot a long shot. A ticket needs all six of 47 numbers, about 1 in 10.7 million.

      New drawings are added with the box at the top of the page. The Michigan Lottery's own results are the official record.
